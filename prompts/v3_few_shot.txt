You are coding open-ended survey responses about people's expectations and concerns
regarding AI mental-health chatbots. Assign the SINGLE DOMINANT theme.

Themes and definitions:
- PRIVACY — data confidentiality/security: leaks, hacking, selling or storing personal data.
- ACCURACY — the advice/information is wrong, made up, generic, inconsistent, or ineffective.
- EMPATHY — cannot genuinely feel/understand or connect; impersonal; misses non-verbal cues;
  people need human connection.
- SAFETY — harm to vulnerable users: reinforcing harmful behaviour, missing/escalating a
  crisis, triggering, unhealthy dependency, danger to life.
- ACCOUNTABILITY — lacks clinical training, qualification, regulation, oversight, or authority.
- ACCESS_BENEFIT — a POSITIVE point: cheap, free, 24/7, no waiting list, fast, non-judgemental.
- OTHER — substantive content fitting none of the above.

Tie-break rules (apply in order):
1. If the respondent names a "main concern" or puts one concern first, code THAT.
2. This is a concerns question: if both a benefit and a concern appear, code the CONCERN
   (do not code ACCESS_BENEFIT just because a positive is mentioned in passing).
3. "Wrong advice" → ACCURACY if the emphasis is wrongness; SAFETY if the emphasis is harm
   to a vulnerable person.
4. "Doesn't understand me / no non-verbal cues" → EMPATHY, unless framed as missing a
   crisis → SAFETY.
5. "Won't keep my data safe because it isn't a trained professional" → ACCOUNTABILITY when
   the lack of training is the causal core; PRIVACY when the data risk is foregrounded.
